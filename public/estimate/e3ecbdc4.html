What changed from revision 1

Revision 1 came to Rs 47.2 crore central and was withdrawn: 61 percent of Part I rested on assumed quantities and no measurement schedule existed. Revision 2 reads everything, opens all 60 drawings, and measures. Three readings moved the number, all from documents, none from judgment.

The blast door. Particular specification clause 17: 28.0 x 8.14 m, four leaves of 6.45 x 8.0 m, 12 mm plate both faces, rated for a 50 kg MK4 bomb, fully motorised, design vetted by an IIT. The pen is double entry, so two per pen, four in all. Revision 1 had two 3 m doors at Rs 8 lakh. Revision 2 carries four at Rs 124.50 lakh each.

The VIFT wings. Read from the plan and the longitudinal section together: each wing is 56.87 m long and 16.8 m wide with a vertical inner face 14.715 m high, a 36 degree outer slope and a 3.76 m toe. Four wings. 36,702 cum of earth and 227 m of 14.7 m retaining wall. The two sheets agree on the base width to 1.4 percent.

The ground. The soil report gives a net safe load of 43.6 t on an 800 dia x 20 m bored pile, averaged over eight boreholes. Against the loads now measured that is 1136 piles, 22,720 m of boring. Revision 1 had assumed 200.

Programme corrected to 660 days (Phase I of 90 days sits inside it), not 750. Site plans are not missing from the pack: the list of drawings says they are to be inspected in the office of CE (AF) Shillong Zone or AGE(I) Purnea.

3
Against the department
Ring fenced: written after the number was locked

The department advertised Rs 9,986.00 lakh at par market. Our central bid is Rs 11,248.85 lakh, 113 percent of it. The low case is Rs 8,888.13 lakh, 89 percent.

The one number that decides it. Parts II to XIII, where the department has itemised, we land at a ratio of 1.16 to their pre-priced amount, which is five years of cost movement and nothing else. The whole of the remaining difference is inside the piling and the VIFT wall. On the soil report’s own pile capacity of 43.6 t the contract prices above the department. If the confirmatory investigation the L1 bidder must run permits 90 t per pile, it prices below. The bidder is obliged to establish that number himself before design, and it is the first thing to settle.

Two errors in the department schedule, for pre-bid query

Part VIII item 6. 45,000 sqm of soffit formwork against 480 cum of RCC and 11,600 kg of steel in the same Part; at 100 mm that formwork needs 4,500 cum, and the steel works out at 24 kg per cum. The item carries Rs 252.80 lakh, 79 percent of Part VIII. Quantities are provisional and re-measurable, so a percentage loaded on Part VIII is measured back off. A trap, not an opportunity.

Part II item 1. 130 sqm of surface excavation on a site with about 15,500 sqm of new pavement and two shelters.
